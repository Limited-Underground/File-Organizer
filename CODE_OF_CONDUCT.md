# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome different experience levels, give constructive technical feedback, and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexual harassment and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation proportionately, explaining decisions where safe. Reports should use the repository's private security-advisory reporting channel when available; do not disclose private details in public issues. The owner must designate a private conduct contact before opening community participation. Maintainers should recuse themselves from reports involving their own conduct; an independent reporting route must be designated for those cases.
